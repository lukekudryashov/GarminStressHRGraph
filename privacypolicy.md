<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

StressHRGraph does not transmit, sell, or share your personal information or Garmin data with the developer or any third party.

StressHRGraph accesses heart-rate zone information from your Garmin user profile when you choose to use Garmin HR zones. This information is used solely on your Garmin device to determine the heart-rate zone displayed by the data field and is not transmitted to or stored by the developer.
Custom settings configured within StressHRGraph are stored as app settings on your Garmin device and are used solely to configure the data field.
StressHRGraph does not collect location data or require an external account.
